Rules of the game

The night runs from 10 PM to 6 AM. One player is secretly the Host, in a pact with the house: they snuff out candles and kill in the dark. Everyone else is a guest. 3 to 10 players; add bots in the lobby.

Guests win if 2+ guests see dawn, if the four pages of the will are read at the Chapel altar, or if every Host is locked in the cellar.

The Host wins by matching the guests in number, or if every hearth goes cold. Dinner is served at midnight, 2 AM and 4 AM, and after each dinner a hearth goes cold.

Your candle is your light, your safety and your clock: refill its wax at a hearth. Nobody can be killed while standing in someone else's light or in a lit room. Report bodies or call a séance to meet and vote someone into the cellar. The dead return as ghosts who can knock and write on mirrors.

  • WASD / mouse: move · E (hold): use · R: report · G: séance · Enter: chat
  • Host: Q (hold): snuff · Space: kill · 1/2: sabotage · E: servant passage
  • Ghost: K: knock · E at a mirror: write a word
